Spider-Man 2 and the Punisher This is a relatively minor link, but during the finale of Sam Raimi's Spider-Man 2, Mary Jane is seen running through the park in her wedding dress and a scowly dude dressed in black turns and looks at her. The commentary track reveals this character is actually the Punisher, and he was inserted as a fun link to the Thomas Jane film that came out in the same year. Legally, they couldn't actually say he was the Punisher or invite Tom Jane to play him, so they used Jane's stunt double instead. Who knows? Spy Kids and Machete Despite both franchises being created by Robert Rodriguez, the Spy Kids and Machete movies couldn't be more different. In the original movie, the kids visit Uncle Machete Cortez at his shop where he makes various gadgets, and it turns out this is what he does in his spare time when he's not a badass secret agent hacking people up with sharp implements. The plot follows Sean Bateman, Patrick's younger brother, during his stint in college. Like his brother, he's not exactly a nice guy, and director Roger Avery wanted to shoot a scene where Patrick and Sean meet.
