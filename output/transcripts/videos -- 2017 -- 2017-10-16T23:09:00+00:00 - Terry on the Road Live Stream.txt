The building of Fort Sumter began in 1829. It is one of a series of coastal fortifications built by the United States after the War of 1812. The original fort walls were built 5 feet thick and stood a towering 50 feet above the waterline. In the early morning hours of April 12, 1861, Confederate cannons opened fire on the fort.
